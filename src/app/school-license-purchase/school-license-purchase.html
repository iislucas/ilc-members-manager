A School License authorizes the individual to open and operate a Licensed School, at one or more locations, in an area that is reserved for their use. They may hire Licensed Instructors to lead classes or events at any of their locations. The holder of a School License does not need to be a Licensed Instructor.

An official School Affiliation License provides:

  • Open & Operate in Reserved Areas: Authorization to open and operate a Licensed School at one or more locations in your reserved territory.
  • Hire Licensed Instructors: Hire Licensed Instructors to lead classes or events at any of your locations (the school license holder does not need to be a licensed instructor).
  • Official School Directory Listing: Listed on the international I Liq Chuan school locator map and directory.
  • Conduct Official Gradings: Authority to host student examinations and submit records to headquarters.
  • Produce and Sell Branded Merchandise: Produce, sell, and distribute official I Liq Chuan branded merchandise.
